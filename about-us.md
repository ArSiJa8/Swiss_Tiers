# About Swisstiers 🇨🇭

**Created by ArSiJa**, Swisstiers is a Swiss version of a Minecraft PvP tier ranking system — similar to mctiers.com, but focused on the Swiss community!

---

## 🔎 Overview
Swisstiers is a Minecraft tier-testing system where you can check your skill across various game modes and see how you rank compared to other players in Switzerland.

---

## 🎮 Testable Game Modes
- Sword  
- UHC  
- SMP  
- Crystal  
- Mace  

---

## 📍 How to Get Tested
To get tested, join our Discord and open a ticket in the **🎟️ tickets** channel. Follow the instructions provided there to start your test.

---

## 🌐 Leaderboard & Ranks
All ranks, leaderboards, and detailed info can be found on our website:  
👉 [swisstiers.arsija.net](https://swisstiers.arsija.net)

---

## ❓ What is a Tier?
A **Tier** represents your skill ranking in a specific game mode based on your PvP performance.  
For example: **Tier 1 to 5**, with High/Low sub-levels.  
Learn more about similar ranking systems on mctiers-like sites.
